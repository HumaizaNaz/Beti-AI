## 🛠️ Step-by-Step AI Tools Guide (Kaise Banayein):

### 1. Voiceover (Aawaz):
* **Tool:** **ElevenLabs.io** (Free tier available).
* **Voice Style:** "Adam" ya "Rachel" (Warm, Serious & Emotional tone).
* Upar diye gaye Roman Urdu / Urdu text ko copy-paste karein aur MP3 download karein.

### 2. Images & Video Clips (Visuals):
* **Image Generator:** **Midjourney / Leonardo.ai / Ideogram** (Upar diye gaye prompts paste karein).
* **Image to Video Generator:** **Runway Gen-3 Alpha / Kling AI / Luma Dream Machine** (Image upload karein aur usko 4-second video clip bana dein).

### 3. Editing & Captions:
* **Tool:** **CapCut** (Mobile / Desktop)
* Video clips aur ElevenLabs voiceover ko timeline par lagayein.
* **Auto Captions** on karein (Bold Yellow/White font with glowing drop shadow).
* Background music mein *"Emotional Cinematic Suspense to Inspiring"* search karein.
