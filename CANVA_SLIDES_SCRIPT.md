### 🟢 Slide 1: Cover Slide
* **Title:** Project Hifazat (Beti Guard AI)
* **Subtitle:** Autonomous, Zero-Touch AI Safety Ecosystem for Every Daughter
* **Tagline:** No App Downloads. No Battery Drain. No Manual Button Presses.
* **Visual / Graphic Idea in Canva:** Modern mobile smartphone with WhatsApp shield icon and glowing pink/indigo AI gradient.

---

### 🔴 Slide 2: The Problem (The Harsh Reality)
* **Heading:** Why 95% of Safety Apps Fail in Real Crises
* **4 Core Points:**
  1. 🔒 **The "Unlock & Press" Delusion:** Attackers snatch phones in seconds. Victims cannot unlock screens to hold SOS buttons.
  2. 🔋 **Extreme Battery Drain:** 24/7 background GPS & mic drains battery in 2–3 hours. Android/iOS kill the app.
  3. 📢 **Alarm Fatigue:** Dumb sound detectors trigger on car horns & laughter, forcing users to uninstall.
  4. 📴 **The "Dead Phone" Blindspot:** If the phone battery hits 0% or is smashed, existing apps go completely silent.
* **Visual Idea:** 4 red/warning warning cards with warning icons.

---

### 💡 Slide 3: The Innovation (Our Solution)
* **Heading:** Introducing Project Hifazat
* **Tagline:** Turning WhatsApp into an Autonomous Guardian
* **3 Pillars:**
  * 💬 **0-Install on WhatsApp:** Zero app download for the girl and her family.
  * ⏱️ **Dead-Man's Switch:** Server-side trip countdown triggers emergency alerts even if the phone dies or is destroyed.
  * 🎙️ **Hands-Free AI Voice:** Recognizes secret distress phrases & panic screams from inside bags.
* **Highlight:** Ultra-Low Power (< 3% battery/day) & 100% Free / Open-Source.

---

### 🔄 Slide 4: How It Works (User Journey)
* **Heading:** 3 Simple Steps to Safety
* **Step 1:** **30-Sec Onboarding:** Save the WhatsApp number, send "Start", and link 2 family emergency contacts.
* **Step 2:** **Natural Trip Logging:** Send a voice note or text: *"Rickshaw KHI-1234, 20 mins to Home"*. AI sets smart timers.
* **Step 3:** **Autonomous Emergency Trigger:** If secret code is spoken or timer expires without safe PIN, instant WhatsApp alerts with live maps & audio are broadcast to family.
